# Currency sample photographs

`php-50.jpg`, `php-100.jpg`, `php-200.jpg`, `php-500.jpg`, `php-1000.jpg`, and `php-20.jpg` are sample images from Roboflow Philippine Banknotes v8. The dataset identifies its license as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Attribution for Roboflow images: “Philippine Banknotes v8” by the Roboflow Philippine Banknotes project, [Roboflow Universe](https://universe.roboflow.com/philippine-banknotes/philippine-banknotes/dataset/8), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

`php-{50,100,500,1000}-polymer-{front,back}.jpg` are user-provided, visibly watermarked SPECIMEN reference images. The demo labels them as specimens and shows front/back views. They are interface examples only and are not included in the AI training dataset.

All sample images are denomination/learning illustrations. They do not establish banknote authenticity, and Roboflow examples may not match the series or material shown in a library entry.
